# How it works

The "Game Predictor" game mode is an interactive quiz where you must predict the winner team of every game in the Round.

Each challenge consists of choosing the winning teams from the 10 games of the Round

The predictions will be made as soon as the new Round is open

You can enter/modify predictions from the start of a Round until 1 minute before the beginning of each individual game of the Round

If the Round has already started, if you have not yet submitted any predictions (e.g., you decide to start playing while the Round is in progress) you can participate by submitting only predictions for games that have not yet begun.

Partial saving is allowed, so you can fill in the quiz partially and return to complete/modify it later.

### GAME STATES

Within the QUIZ section, for each Round, you can choose the teams who will win in each game.

Below is how the Round progresses and the actions you can take in each state:

Open Quiz: You can make your picks for each game. New games are available as soon as the new Round opens. You can freely modify your choices until each game begins.

Quiz in Progress: The Round has started. You can change your selections only for the games that still have to start, and you can follow the real-time scores.

Ranking Calculation: Round is over, and the final rankings are being calculated.

Round Finished: The Round has ended, and you can view your final score while waiting for the new predictions to be made available.&#x20;

### SCORING SYSTEM

For each correct prediction you get 10 points.

The sum of the points accumulated in each Round determines your position in the Rankings.&#x20;
