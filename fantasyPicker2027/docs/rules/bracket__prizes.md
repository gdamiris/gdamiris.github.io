# Prizes

## EuroLeague Prizes

* Grand Prize: two (2) 2027 Final Four Tickets
* Silver Prize: four (4) EuroLeague tickets for the 2026-27 regular season, for any game(s) selected by the winner
* Bronze Prize: one (1) EuroLeagueTV Annual Pass for the 2026-27 season

## BKT EuroCup Prizes

* Grand Prize: four (4) BKT EuroCup tickets for the 2026-27 regular season, for any game(s) selected by the winner
* Silver Prize: one (1) EuroLeague TV Annual Pass for the 2026-27 season
* Bronze Prize: one (1) EuroCup Spalding Basketball
