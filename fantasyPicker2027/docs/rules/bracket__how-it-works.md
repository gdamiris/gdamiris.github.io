# How it works

## 1. HOW TO PARTICIPATE

The game opens at the end of the Regular Season and before the tip-off of the first Play-in game!

### 1.1 Registration

To join the Bracket Challenge, you must have a EuroLeague ID.

* If you already have an account on the EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge app or web version, simply log in.
* If you don’t, sign up for free at[ EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge](https://euroleaguefantasy.euroleaguebasketball.net/) and create your [EuroLeague ID](https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/euroleague/euroleague-id/) account.
* On the EuroLeague or EuroCup homepage, look for the Bracket Challenge promotional banner and click to access the game.

### 1.2 Creating Your Bracket

* You can create up to 3 brackets per account
* Brackets must be submitted before the first game of the competition (the first Play-in game for the EuroLeague bracket or the first Playoff game for the EuroCup bracket).
* Once created, you can freely modify your bracket until the start of the first game.&#x20;
* After the competition begins, you will no longer be able to edit existing brackets or create new ones.

## 2. GAME STRUCTURE

### 2.1 EuroCup

The Bracket Challenge consists of the following rounds:

**Playoffs (Single Game Rounds)**

* Round of 16 (Single game) → Winners advance to the Quarterfinals.
* Quarterfinals (Single game) → Winners move to the Semifinals.

**Semifinals (Best-of-3 Series)**

* The first team to win 2 games moves on to the Final.

**Final (Best-of-3 Series)**

* The first team to win 2 games is crowned EuroCup Champion!

### 2.2 EuroLeague

The Bracket Challenge consists of the following rounds:

**Play-In Tournament (Single Game Rounds)**

* Play-In A: 7th seed vs. 8th seed → Winner advances to the Quarterfinals
* Play-In B: 9th seed vs. 10th seed → Loser is eliminated, winner advances.
* Final Play-In: Loser of Play-In A vs. Winner of Play-In B → The winner moves to the Quarterfinals, while the loser is eliminated.

**Playoffs (Best-of-5 Series)**

* Quarterfinal matchups are determined based on regular season rankings.
* The first team to win 3 games moves on to the Final Four.

**Final Four (Single Game Rounds)**

* Semifinals: The four remaining teams face off in single-elimination games.
* Finals: The two semifinal winners play a single game to determine the EuroLeague Champion.

## 3. HOW TO PLAY

### 3.1 Making Your Predictions

For each matchup, you can make your own predictions by selecting from the available options. Please note that, depending on the round, some or all of the following predictions may be available:

* Winner of each game/series.
* Exact Result of the Series
* Final score difference (for the final)
* Finals MVP

### 3.2 Scoring System

**EuroLeague**

| Prediction                        | Points |
| --------------------------------- | ------ |
| Winner of Play Ins                | 10     |
| Winner Series (Playoffs)          | 15     |
| Correct Result Series (e.g.: 3-1) | 5      |
| Winner Single Game - Semi         | 20     |
| Final Winner                      | 50     |
| Final MVP                         | 10     |
| Final Points Gap                  | 5      |

**EuroCup**

| Prediction                                | Points |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Winner of Eightfinals                     | 10     |
| Winner of Quarterfinals                   | 15     |
| Winner of  Semifinals                     | 20     |
| Correct Result Series - Semi (e.g.: 2-1)  | 5      |
| Final Winner                              | 50     |
| Final MVP                                 | 10     |
| Correct Result Series - Final (e.g.: 2-1) | 5      |

## 4. LEADERBOARDS

#### 4.1 Public Leagues

Each Bracket is automatically registered in a **Global league**.

In addition to these main leagues, you can join and/or create other leagues:

* **Private leagues**: generally between friends, accessible only by an invitation code
* **Public leagues**: open to anyone

In all these leagues, teams are ranked based on the scores achieved in the different Rounds. In case of a tie, the user who submitted their bracket first will be ranked higher.

## 5. GAME RULES & REGULATIONS

* **Fair Play**: The organizer reserves the right to disqualify entries that violate the terms & conditions.
* **Organizer's Rights**: The organizer reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the game at any time, as well as to make final decisions on rankings, disputes, and any unforeseen circumstances.
* **Technical Issues**: The organizer’s system is the official timekeeper. Any late entries, failed submissions, or disputes will be handled at their discretion.
