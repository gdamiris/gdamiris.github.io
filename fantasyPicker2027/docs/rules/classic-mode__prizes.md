# Prizes

*Amazing prizes are available to be won throughout the season.*

*All prizes are personal, non-transferable and may not be exchanged for any alternative prize.*

*Each participant is eligible to receive only one prize throughout the competition. In the event that a participant qualifies for multiple prizes across different competitions, challenges or rankings, they will be awarded only the highest-value prize for which they qualify. The corresponding prize(s) from the remaining competition(s) will be reallocated to the next highest-ranked eligible participant(s), in accordance with the final standings.*

Here are the prizes for the two competitions:

* [**EuroLeague**](#euroleague)
* [**BKT EuroCup**<br>](#bkt-eurocup)
