# Suspended or changed games

#### ANTICIPATED OR POSTPONED GAMES

* If the games are moved to a time in which the previous Round has already ended and the upcoming Round has not started yet, then they are considered valid for the game.
* If the games are moved to a time when the previous Round has not ended yet or the next Round has already started, then at the end of the first game turn (T1) the players of the teams involved (including coaches, injured and disqualified players) will get their **Average Score** (i.e. the average score obtained up to that point of the season).
* Note:
  * &#x20;In case of postponed matches - where teams are put at rest - and average score awarded, the captain will NOT double his score
  * In the case of a canceled game during an open Round, the captain will keep his score doubled

#### SPECIAL CASES

In case of special events (postponement of many games or a whole Round), the organization reserves the right to make any decision, which will be promptly communicated through apps, news and social channels.
