# CLASSIC MODE

EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge offers two game modes: Classic (all versus all with non-exclusive rosters) or Draft (private leagues with exclusive rosters defined by an auction).

In this section, you will find the features of Classic Mode.

For more details on the Draft mode, please read the dedicated section.

Up to 3 fantasy teams: 100 credits to create each fantasy team consisting of 10 players and 1 head coach.

Non-exclusive rosters: You can choose any player (within the budget), even if they have already been selected by other users.

Private Leagues: Your teams automatically participate in the General League against all users of the game, but you can also create private leagues (in General ranking mode or Head-to-Head mode) to challenge your friends.

Scores: The players on your fantasy team earn a score based on real statistics recorded in the EuroLeague championship. The starting five players, the sixth man, and the coach get 100% of the points, while players on the bench get 50%.

Captain and Bench: Select a Captain from the starting five players; their score will be multiplied by 2x.

Schedule: Each Round is divided into Turns, which correspond to the block of games played on a single day. Between one Turn and the next, you can change formation, captain, and make field-bench substitutions.

Field-Bench Substitutions: During a Round, you can make field-bench substitutions, change formation, and switch Captain.

Trades: At the end of each Round, you can buy and sell players to improve your fantasy team.

Prizes: Amazing prizes are available to be won for all participants.
