**NOTES:**&#x20;

* You will be able to access the **Post Season** game via the top dropdown menu, similar to how you switch between EuroLeague and EuroCup. From there, you can manage your Post Season teams and leagues. Your **Regular Season** tab will remain active for viewing past results and standings.

<figure><img src="/files/6pMF657zIGnGf2tFGusf" alt="" width="323"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Players on teams not playing in a given Round will score 0 points, but you will have unlimited trades during every Round.**
